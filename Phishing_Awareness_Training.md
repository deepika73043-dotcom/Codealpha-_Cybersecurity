

# Phishing Awareness Training
## CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship

**Objective:** To understand phishing attacks, identify warning signs, and learn prevention techniques.

### 1. Introduction
Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers use fake messages or websites to steal sensitive information.

### 2. Warning Signs
- Urgent or threatening messages
- Suspicious links and fake domains
- Requests for passwords or OTPs
- Unknown attachments
- Fake prizes and offers

### 3. Example
A fictional email says: "Your account will be blocked today. Click this link and enter your password."

Warning signs: urgency, an unknown link, and a request for a password.

### 4. Social Engineering
Attackers may impersonate banks, companies, or technical support to gain trust and manipulate users.

### 5. Prevention
- Never share passwords or OTPs.
- Verify website addresses.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Avoid suspicious attachments.
- Report phishing messages.

### 6. Quick Quiz
**Q1:** Should you click an unexpected link threatening to block your account?

Answer: No. Verify through an official channel.

**Q2:** Should you share your OTP with someone claiming to be bank support?

Answer: Never.

### 7. Conclusion
Awareness, verification, and safe online practices help protect users from phishing attacks.
